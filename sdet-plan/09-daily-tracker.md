## If you fall behind

Do not restart the plan or try to catch up on everything. Reprioritise in this
order and let the rest go:

1. **Applications** — the pipeline never pauses. This is first, always.
2. **Repo #1 (API framework)** — the single highest-value artifact
3. **Python core: OOP, decorators, generators, pytest fixtures** — the four
   topics that appear in every interview
4. **Your differentiator stories** — IoT performance and security, rehearsed
   with numbers
5. Everything else

Weeks 3 and 4 can stretch to six weeks without damage. Weeks 1 and 2 cannot be
skipped — they are what the interviews are actually testing.
